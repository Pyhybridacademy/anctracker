Locating Your Code

How to Find Your Tracking Number

Your tracking code is available in several places after your shipment is registered.

01

Shipping Receipt

Printed on the receipt you received when dropping off your package — usually at the top or bottom.

02

Email Confirmation

Check the confirmation email sent after your shipment was registered. Search your inbox for our logistics team.

03

SMS Notification

If you provided a phone number, you should have received an SMS with your tracking code and confirmation details.

04

Customer Account

Log into your account to view all your shipments and their tracking codes in your order history dashboard.

Quick Tips for Tracking

Case Sensitive: Enter your tracking code exactly as shown, including capitalisation.

No Spaces: Remove any spaces between characters in your tracking code.

Update Frequency: Tracking information is updated every 2–4 hours during transit.
